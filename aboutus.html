We are a non profit organisation belonging to charitable trust. we acts as a bridge between needy people and a donar,we ensure you that our each member works selflessly and takes full responsibility that your donated items gets to the needed person.
Our Goals

Our main goal is to provide necessary items to many children as possible.The items that we collect from you is reached to children by conducting charaitabe events on timely basis.Many people volunteer in these events.


How can you help us

You can help us in two ways by donating the necessary items and by volunteering the events.Both process is explained in detail below.


Donating Process

You can donate bags,shoes,toys and clothes.You just need to fill the form and upload the photos of your items and within a week you can expect your volunteer member to your doorstep and collecting the items.We request you to put all the items in a cardboard box.


Volunteering Process

You can volunteer for the events that held in our trust by registering the volunteer form and we will notify you when we conduct any events over email.After volunteering you will get CharitAble merchandise.
